---
term: Prompt template
definition: "A prompt with named slots for the parts that change each time (topic, audience, source text) and fixed text for the parts that must not change (constraints, format, quality checks). A template is a small artefact with a job: reuse a prompt that worked without retyping it, and keep its rules from drifting between uses."
