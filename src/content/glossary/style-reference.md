term: Style reference
definition: "An image (or its written description) used to guide the look of a new image — palette, light, texture, mood. References transfer attributes reliably, not exact compositions or likenesses; the craft is naming the attributes you are taking. Treat a reference as an influence to decompose and combine, not as a copy button."
