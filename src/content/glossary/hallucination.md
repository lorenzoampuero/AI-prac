term: Hallucination
definition: "A hallucination is a fluent, confident AI answer — or a detail inside one — that is not true. Because models generate what plausibly comes next rather than checking facts, fabrications cluster around specifics: numbers, dates, names, quotes and citations. The practical response is verification, not distrust of everything."
