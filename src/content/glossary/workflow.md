term: Workflow
definition: "A fixed sequence you design — inputs, actions, outputs and checks — with AI steps placed inside a structure you control. A workflow does not choose its own path: the order is authored, so its cost, failure points and review gates are predictable. When a job has known steps and a checkable output, a workflow beats both a single mega-prompt and an open-ended agent."
