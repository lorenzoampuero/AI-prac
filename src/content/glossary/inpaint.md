---
term: Inpaint
definition: "Editing only part of an image: you mark a region (a mask) and describe what should replace what is there. Tools call this inpainting or region editing, and it is how adjustments and removals are made without re-rolling the whole picture. The mask guides the model rather than constraining it exactly — describe the replacement clearly and inspect the result closely."
